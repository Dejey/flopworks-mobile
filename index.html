Character creator

Paint straight onto the head: hair, a hat, a beard, scars, a whole new face. Switch to Clothes to paint the top, the sleeves, the legs and the shoes on the body laid out flat. Cloth is one loose piece (a cape, a scarf, a skirt, long hair) that hangs from the body and swings: paint it top to bottom, pick what it hangs from. Clothes paint may go a little outside the body (cuffs, a skirt, boot tops): it stays on the nearest part and moves with it. For a collar, paint inside the dashed collar box under the chin: it is drawn in front of the neck and the bottom of the head. Collision makes whatever you painted sticking out of the head solid. Fill pours the colour into a whole area at once. There is room round the head for anything that sticks out. The painting faces right and turns with the dummy.

FLOPWORKS

A ragdoll sandbox. Drop characters in, give them a mode, then mess with them.

  • Tools (left): grab, pin, rope, weld, blade, cannon, bombs, mines, rockets, balloons, props, walls, saws, fans, bouncers, portals — and pourable water, lava and acid that really flow (build walls to hold them).
  • Powers (right): blast, black hole, lightning, fire, freeze, tornado, telekinesis, slow-mo, size ray, heal, meteor, quake, sky laser, lift, rain, sleep, rage, clone.
  • Characters (bottom): 9 ragdolls with different bodies, stats and a signature special (the last one, Zed, is a zombie) move.
  • Teams (bottom right): put ragdolls on Red, Blue, Green or Yellow (the selected one, or everyone if none is selected). Teammates never attack or hurt each other and rush to defend a teammate who gets hit. Opposing teams talk trash: taunts in a fight, insults when they meet.
  • Maps (top bar): Lab, OG Map (the original starting scene), Playground, Volcano, Water Park, Sky Towers, Moon Base, Gauntlet. Reset rebuilds the current map.
  • Modes: Limp, Stand, Walk, Fight, Zombie, Dance, Flee, Possess. Grab a ragdoll to select it — modes apply to the selected one, or to everyone if none is selected.
  • Possess: A/D move · W/Space jump · S crouch · F punch · C grab / let go (then F throws) · G kick · E special · Q go limp
  • H heal the dummy under the cursor · M pause (tools still work while paused, so you can set things up) · T slow-mo · R reset · N mute · The view starts at 1.2× zoom: Z zooms in · J zooms out · double-tap Z goes back to 1.2× · push the cursor to an edge to pan · right-click = alt action (shrink / cancel) · ⚙ settings: vitals panel, NPCs socializing, NPCs reacting (to each other and to disasters), realistic falling, blood and bruises, full destruction (smash any body part into paste)
